# 12.6.1 Real-watch checklist

Task 12.6.1 (docs/23): closes every "verify on device" item for the Watch Companion [PRD §8]. Follow it with a physical iPhone and a physical Apple Watch. The simulator can't check any of this.

Each item lists its sources, setup, steps, what you should see or feel, and pass/fail boxes. Write anything odd under **Notes**: a timing, a screenshot name, a log line.

---

## Before you start

**Devices**

| Need | For |
|---|---|
| iPhone on iOS 18 or later, paired with an Apple Watch on watchOS 11 or later | Most of the checklist, the Smart Stack (§8) and Live Activity on iOS 18 (§7b) |
| iPhone on iOS 17 (any 17.x) | Live Activity on iOS 17 (§7a). Skip it if you can't get one, and record that it was skipped. |
| Apple Watch 40 mm or 41 mm (the smallest you can get) | Screens at the smallest size (§9). The simulator captures in `docs/design/watch-screens/se3-40mm/` are the reference. |

**Install:** run the Stabilyz scheme from Xcode on the iPhone, or use a TestFlight build. Check that the watch app installs too: on the iPhone, open Watch → My Watch → Available Apps (or Installed on Apple Watch).

**Defaults unless a step says otherwise**
- Setup screen: Quick Test (1:30).
- Start & Stop Haptics: on.
- Step Feedback and Metronome: off.
- Phone volume up.
- iPhone and watch on the same wrist side, within arm's reach.

**Reading the phone's logs (optional, helps several items):** on a Mac, open Console.app, select the iPhone, and filter on subsystem `com.adi.Stabilyz` and category `session`. Useful lines:
- `countdown: haptics owned by the watch` / `… by the phone`: which device owns the haptics for this session.
- `countdown: watch missed Go; the phone played it`
- `watch: stop unconfirmed; the phone played the Stop pulse`
- `watch: stop requested from the watch` / `watch: stop request dropped`
- `watch nudge: shown` / `watch nudge: Live Activities are off`

**Ways to make the watch unreachable**
- Walk out of Bluetooth range with the watch while the phone stays behind.
- Put the watch in Airplane Mode (Control Center).
- Force-quit the watch app (press the side button, swipe the app away).

Use whichever the step names. Wi-Fi can keep a watch reachable at longer range, so for range tests turn Wi-Fi off on the phone.

**Haptics are felt, not seen.** When a step says "feel", keep the phone in a front trouser pocket and the watch on your wrist, and say out loud which device buzzed.

### 4.5 Stop from the watch while the watch app is in the background
*Sources: PRD OQ-7c; decisions.md entry 54; Apple WatchConnectivity `isReachable` and `sendMessage` documentation*

- **Steps:**
  1. Right after Go, lower your wrist (screen off), or press the Digital Crown to leave the app.
  2. Raise your wrist and tap Stop on the watch, so the watch app comes back from the background. Repeat with the app left in the background for the whole walk, if the watch lets you reach Stop another way.
